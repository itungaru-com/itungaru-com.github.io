Our story

A little piece of Kiribati, wherever you are.

I-Tungaru means a person of Tungaru — the old name for the islands of Kiribati. This shop is for the I-Kiribati scattered across the world, and for anyone who has fallen for the islands.

We keep the designs simple on purpose: one mark per item, drawn from the things that make the islands what they are. The frigatebird, gliding over the lagoon. The sun, rising out of the Pacific. The tides that shape every day.

We're just getting started. The first tees, hats, magnets and more are here now, with new designs to come.
